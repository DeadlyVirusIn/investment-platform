# ArthOS — 10-Minute Demo Script

**Goal:** full walkthrough for an investor meeting — establish the problem, demo the loop end to end, prove trustworthiness, and pre-empt the hard questions. Run on the seeded demo device (real numbers: +5.21%, 62 resolved, ~50% win-rate, 30 open). Desktop primary; phone for the "it works everywhere" beat. Keep ~2 min for live Q&A. Avoid `/v2/options` diagnostics and "not yet connected" pages.

## 1. Framing (0:00–1:00) — *Why ArthOS exists*
"Two ways people meet investing today: a trader terminal — charts, options chains, real money up front — or a tip, from a finfluencer or now ChatGPT, that just says 'buy this.' Both fail a beginner: one overwhelms, the other asks for blind trust. So people hold cash or gamble. ArthOS is the third way — a copilot that teaches you to invest by walking you through one real idea a day, safely." *Show the landing + trust strip (Plain English · Paper-only · Your own proof).*

## 2. Discover → Top Idea (1:00–2:15) — *The daily surface*
*Open Discover, click today's top Buy.*
"Every day the engine surfaces ideas with a real plan — entry, target, exit, timeframe — and a one-line **Why now**: what changed that makes this worth attention today. A beginner sees what to do and why it matters, not a wall of indicators. Note the plain English: no SMA, no RSI on this surface."

## 3. Bull vs Bear (2:15–3:45) — *The flagship; why we're different*
*Scroll to the Bull-vs-Bears card.*
"This is the heart of it. For every idea: why bulls like it, why bears worry, and then **where ArthOS lands and why**. Three supportive signals, two risks, and an honest verdict — including that we *trimmed* the call for volatility rather than taking it at face value. ChatGPT gives you one confident answer; ArthOS gives you the argument, including the case against itself. That's the difference between a chatbot and a copilot you can trust."

## 4. See the working (3:45–4:45) — *Why it's trustworthy*
*Expand "See the working".*
"And nothing is hidden. This is the real audit trail — eight signals across the families it weighs, what helped the ranking, what hurt it, what it considered and set aside, and the exact adjustments. A skeptical analyst can verify the whole call. Try getting that out of a black-box model."

## 5. Add to paper → My Portfolio (4:45–5:45) — *Safe by construction*
*Use 'Add to paper', then open My Portfolio.*
"Following an idea puts it in a **paper** account — practice money, nothing real at stake. Here's the book: value, cash, unrealized and realized P/L, all from real prices, 'source: live'. There is no live-trading path in this product — paper-only is structural, not a setting. That's also our regulatory posture: education, not advice."

## 6. Track Record Integrity Card (5:45–7:00) — *Why it's trustworthy, part 2*
*Open Track Record → Integrity Card.*
"Here's the part diligence loves. Realized return, realized P/L, drawdown, average hold — every figure computed from real paper trades and live snapshots, nothing hand-entered. And look: we **refuse to publish a win-rate until ten ideas resolve**. Honesty is a design constraint — we'd rather show 'hidden' than a number we can't back. Every number here is scoped to this user's book and reproducible; we ran a full technical due-diligence pass to confirm it."

## 7. Reflection Loop (7:00–8:00) — *Why users stay*
*Scroll to Reflections.*
"When an idea closes, the system grades itself — what we expected, what happened, what we learned — built from the real recorded outcome, not after-the-fact commentary. This stop-loss did its job; this one, the engine reversed its own call when the evidence changed. That's the retention engine: users watch the copilot, and themselves, get better over time."

## 8. Methodology + Workflow (8:00–9:00) — *Why it's hard to copy*
*Open Methodology, show the workflow diagram.*
"One pipeline, end to end — market data, analysis, both sides, a recommendation, your paper portfolio, an honest track record, reflection. The moat isn't one model anyone can clone. It's the **discipline** — both sides on every call, gated metrics, paper-only — and the **per-user proof that compounds**. A competitor can copy a screen; they can't copy a year of a user's audited track record, or the willingness to show the downside on every idea."

## 9. Close + the five answers (9:00–10:00)
"To recap: ArthOS **exists** to make investing learnable; it's **different** because it shows both sides and its working; it's **trustworthy** because every number is real, gated, and auditable; users **stay** because the reflection loop makes them better; and it's **hard to copy** because the moat is honesty plus per-user proof, not a model. Everyone else is racing to let AI trade more with real money. We're building the one a beginner can trust." *Hand to Q&A; keep the FAQ answers ready.*
