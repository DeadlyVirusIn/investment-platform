# ArthOS — 3-Minute Demo Script

**Goal:** in 3 minutes, land *different + trustworthy*. Run on the seeded demo device (real numbers). Avoid `/v2/options` diagnostics and "not yet connected" pages. Works desktop or phone.

## 0:00–0:25 — The hook (Landing)
*Show the landing hero + trust strip.*
"Most people never learn to invest — the tools are built for traders or they just say 'buy this'. ArthOS is a beginner's AI investing copilot. One real idea a day, in plain English, nothing real at stake. We don't promise returns — we promise literacy." *Point at: Plain English · Paper-only · Your own proof.*

## 0:25–1:10 — The flagship (Bull vs Bear)
*Open today's top idea, scroll to the Bull-vs-Bears card.*
"Here's the whole thesis. ChatGPT gives you a confident paragraph. ArthOS shows you **both sides** — why bulls like it, why bears worry — and then where it lands and why. It shows the case *against* its own call. That's a copilot you can trust, not a hype machine."

## 1:10–1:45 — The receipts (See the working)
*Expand "See the working".*
"And it's not a black box. This is the actual audit trail — every signal it weighed, what helped the ranking, what hurt it, and how the call was trimmed. An investor can verify the reasoning end to end."

## 1:45–2:35 — The proof (Track Record + Reflection)
*Open Track Record → Integrity Card, then the Reflection Loop.*
"Every call is recorded — real return, realized P/L, drawdown — all from real paper trades. We won't even show a win-rate until ten ideas resolve; honesty is built in. And when an idea closes, the system grades itself: what we expected, what happened, what we learned. That loop is why people stay — they watch the copilot, and themselves, get better."

## 2:35–3:00 — The close
"Everyone's racing to let AI trade more, faster, with real money. We're building the one a beginner can trust, audit, and learn from — paper-only by construction, honest by design, with per-user proof that compounds. That's the moat, and it's live today."

## Notes
