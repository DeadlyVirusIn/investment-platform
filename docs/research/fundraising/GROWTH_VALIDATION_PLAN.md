# ArthOS — Growth & Validation Plan (demand-proof only)

**Date:** 2026-06-20 · Written as YC partner + seed-stage fintech founder + product growth lead. Premise: funding depends entirely on **proving demand in 30 days**. No new features, no UI, no product work. The job is distribution, recruitment, and measurement. Ruthless.

## The one-sentence truth

ArthOS has a built, audited product and **zero evidence anyone wants it.** Every hour spent on product in the next 30 days is malpractice. Spend the month doing things that don't scale: hand-recruit a narrow cohort, run a weekly-habit loop, and measure return + willingness-to-pay.

## 1. What evidence of pull is missing today

Everything that matters: any real users; activation (did they follow an idea / see a reflection); **weekly return** (the only metric that counts); organic acquisition (did anyone arrive unprompted); referral/share behavior; willingness-to-pay; and qualitative PMF signal ("I'd be very disappointed without this"). The seeded demo book is not demand. A great demo is not demand. **Retention is demand.**

## 2. The 10 highest-signal metrics investors will ask for

1. **W1 / W4 retention** by cohort (the headline — do they come back unprompted).
2. **WAU/MAU stickiness** (target >25–30% as a habit signal).
3. **Activation rate** — signup → first "aha" (followed first paper idea OR saw first self-graded reflection).
4. **Week-over-week growth** of activated users.
5. **Organic vs paid mix** (CAC ≈ $0 channels = the real signal pre-seed).
6. **Referral / share rate** (k-factor, even anecdotal).
7. **PMF survey:** % "very disappointed without ArthOS" (Sean-Ellis; target ≥40%).
8. **Sessions per active user per week** (frequency = habit).
9. **Waitlist → activation conversion.**
10. **Willingness-to-pay signal** — a pre-order, paid pilot, or paywall conversion (even N=5 paying beats 5,000 free).

## 3. Which are realistically achievable in 30 days

- **Yes:** signups, activation rate, **W1 retention (one cohort)**, sessions/week, PMF survey on small N, 15–20 user interviews, organic-share anecdotes, a small **WTP probe** (LOI / pre-order / Stripe link).
- **No (need time / scale):** W4–W8 retention *curves*, statistically significant cohorts, meaningful revenue. Don't promise these in 30 days — promise W1 + activation + qualitative + a WTP signal, and the *start* of a W4 curve.

## 4. Smallest experiment that proves weekly return

**The concierge daily-idea loop.** Hand-pick 25–50 target users into a 2-week cohort. Each weekday, push the single "today's idea" (email/DM/notification) and watch behavior — no new product, just the existing daily surface plus a manual nudge. Success bar: **≥35–40% return on ≥3 of 5 weekdays in week 1, and ≥30% return in week 2 unprompted.** Even smaller pre-test: manually DM 20 people the daily idea and count how many ask for tomorrow's. If they ask, you have a loop. If you have to chase them, you don't.

## 5. Fastest path to 25 / 100 / 500 users

- **25 (week 1):** founder-led hand recruitment — personal network, 50 cold DMs to the target persona, one finance creator's comments/audience, one targeted post in a beginner-investing community. Concierge onboarding each one personally.
- **100 (weeks 2–4):** one **finance-creator partnership** (they post the both-sides / "AI shows why it's wrong" format), plus a coordinated push to 2–3 beginner-investing communities + a waitlist/landing with a sharp hook. Manual onboarding still.
- **500 (60–90 days):** a repeatable **content loop** (the self-grading-calls + both-sides format on TikTok/X/Reddit is the unit), 3–5 creator partnerships, and a referral mechanic. 500 is a *channel* result, not a hustle result — it requires one channel that demonstrably works, found in the first 30 days.

## 6. Which cohort to target first — ruthless pick

**Young adults (≈22–35) who recently opened a brokerage account but feel lost.** Why this slice and not the others:

- **Beginner investors (broad):** correct persona but un-findable and low-intent as a mass — too diffuse to recruit or retain in 30 days.
- **College students:** findable via clubs, but low WTP and they churn at semester end — bad retention/revenue signal for fundraising.
- **Finance creators:** NOT users — they're the **distribution channel.** Partner, don't target.
- **Bank employees / World Bank / IFC:** that's the *B2B/credibility* track (slow, enterprise) — it does not prove consumer pull in 30 days. Run it in parallel for LOIs, not for the demand metric.

The chosen slice is **high-intent** (they already acted — opened an account — so motivation is proven), **findable** (they cluster in creator audiences and beginner-investing communities), and **exactly ArthOS's persona** (acted, now overwhelmed). Reach them *through* finance creators. One narrow, reachable, motivated cohort beats a broad vague one every time.

## 7. "Interesting product" vs "people actually want this"

- **"Interesting product"** = a great demo, a launch-day signup spike, compliments, a press hit. All vanity, all decaying.
- **"People actually want this"** = **they come back weekly without being prompted**, they tell other people (organic arrivals/referrals), a meaningful share say they'd be "very disappointed" without it, and at least a few **pay or commit**. The line is **retention + organic pull + WTP**, never signups. An investor flips from "interesting" to "want" the moment your retention curve flattens above zero and someone you never met shows up.

## 30-day traction plan (proof of weekly return)

- **Week 1:** instrument the funnel (lightweight: signup → activation → return events; manual tracking is fine for the first cohort — this is measurement, not product). Hand-recruit the first **25** of the target persona; concierge-onboard each.
- **Week 2:** launch the concierge daily-idea loop to the cohort; start the PMF survey and 15 user interviews; stand up a sharp waitlist/landing.
- **Week 3:** close one **creator partnership**; push to **100**; run the WTP probe (pre-order / paid-pilot ask / Stripe link).
- **Week 4:** read **W1 retention + activation + sessions/week + PMF %**; write the one-page traction memo. Decision: is the loop real?
- **Targets:** ≥100 activated; **W1 ≥35%**; ≥40% "very disappointed"; ≥5 WTP commitments; ≥3 unprompted organic arrivals.

## 60-day traction plan (find the channel)

- Double down on whichever acquisition channel worked in month 1; kill the rest.
- Scale to **250–500** activated; get the **first W4 retention** read on the month-1 cohort.
- Convert the WTP probe into a small **paid pilot** (even N=10 paying) or a creator-driven paid cohort.
- **Targets:** W4 ≥25%; WAU/MAU ≥25%; one channel with repeatable CAC ≈ $0; first revenue dollars or a signed B2B design-partner LOI.

## 90-day traction plan (fundraising-ready)

- Turn the working channel into a repeatable **content/referral engine**; **500+** activated, growing WoW.
- A **W4 retention curve that flattens** (cohorts holding, not decaying to zero).
- **≥40% PMF**, evidence of organic acquisition, and a real WTP/revenue or LOI line.
- **Outcome:** the deck's weakest slide (traction) becomes its strongest. Now raise.

## Minimum traction milestone before fundraising (the bar)

**Do not raise a priced round below this:** ~**300–500 weekly-active** target-persona users, **W4 retention ≥25–30% that has flattened**, **≥40% "very disappointed"** on a real-N PMF survey, **demonstrable organic acquisition** (a channel, not just hustle), and **at least one willingness-to-pay signal** (paying users or a B2B design-partner LOI). Hit that and you're raising on proof, not potential. Miss it and you're asking investors to fund a hypothesis at a valuation it can't carry — take a small founder-friendly pre-seed instead and come back when the curve flattens.

## The ruthless summary

The product is done; the company is not started until someone returns next week unprompted. For 30 days: no features, no polish, no decks. Recruit a narrow high-intent cohort by hand, run the daily-idea loop, and measure return + WTP. One flattened retention curve from real users is worth more than every screen you could build.
