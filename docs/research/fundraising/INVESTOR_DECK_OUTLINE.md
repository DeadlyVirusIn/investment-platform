# ArthOS — Investor Deck Outline

**Format:** 12 core slides + appendix. Each slide: one headline idea, one visual, one line of proof. Built for a 10-minute pitch. Demo metrics are real (seeded paper book: NAV ~$105K, +5.21%, 30 open, 62 resolved, ~50% win-rate, accuracy published 10/10).

## 1. Title
ArthOS — "The AI investing copilot that teaches you to invest." Beginner-first, paper-only, honest by design. One line, logo, the product on a phone.

## 2. Problem
Most people never learn to invest. The tools are built for traders (charts, jargon, real money up front) or they're opaque tip-sheets ("buy this"). Beginners get either overwhelm or blind trust. Result: a generation that holds cash or gambles. *Visual: a Robinhood options chain next to a ChatGPT "should I buy NVDA?" answer — both fail a beginner.*

## 3. Solution
ArthOS walks a beginner through one real idea a day, in plain English — shows both sides, shows its working, and tracks the outcome in a paper account. You learn how an investor thinks by watching the AI think, with nothing real at stake. *Visual: the Bull-vs-Bear card.*

## 4. Why now
LLMs finally make plain-English, auditable financial reasoning cheap; retail investing is mainstream but financial literacy hasn't caught up; regulators are tightening on "finfluencer" advice — a paper-only, education-framed, transparent product is the safe lane. *Visual: three converging trends.*

## 5. Product — the loop
Discover → Learn → Practice, closed by Reflection. Show the four flagship surfaces: Bull vs Bear, See-the-working (Trace), Track Record Integrity Card, Reflection Loop. *Visual: the Methodology workflow diagram (Market data → Analysis → Bull/Bear → Recommendation → Paper portfolio → Track record → Reflection).*

## 6. Differentiation — vs ChatGPT, vs Robinhood
ChatGPT gives a confident paragraph; ArthOS gives the case *against* its own call, the audit trail, and a track record that grades itself. Robinhood monetizes trading volume; ArthOS monetizes literacy and has no incentive to make you trade. *Visual: 3-column compare (ChatGPT / Robinhood / ArthOS).*

## 7. Moat
Not one model — an honest, beginner-safe loop plus **per-user proof that compounds**. The moat is the discipline (both sides on every call, win-rate gated until N resolves, paper-only by construction) and the accruing per-user track record competitors can't clone. *Visual: the 7-pillar moat (see TECHNICAL_MOAT_PLAN).*
