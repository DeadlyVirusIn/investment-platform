# The 5-Minute Investor Demo Flow

**Date:** 2026-06-20 · Task 4. The single best 5-minute walkthrough for investor / innovation-fund meetings. Run on the seeded demo device (NAV ~$105K, 30 open, 62 resolved, +5.21%, accuracy published 10/10). Each beat names the surface, what to show, what to say, and which of the five questions it answers.

## Setup (before the call)
- Demo device with the cloned book; verify Discover shows a Buy top idea and "updated today / source: live".
- Avoid `/v2/options` (diagnostics) and the "not yet connected" pages (FieldNotes / Catalysts / Watchlist).
- Have both desktop (1440) and a phone (391) ready — the flow is verified on both.

## Beat 1 — Landing (0:00–0:30) · *Why does ArthOS exist?*
Show the onboarding hero + trust strip. Say: "Most people never learn to invest because the tools are built for traders. ArthOS is a beginner-first AI investing **copilot** — it walks you through one real idea a day, in plain English, with nothing real at stake. We don't promise returns; we promise literacy." Point at the trust strip: **Plain English · Paper-only · Your own proof.**

## Beat 2 — Discover → Top Idea (0:30–1:15) · *Why is it different?*
Open Discover ("Ideas you can follow and prove"), click today's top Buy. Say: "Every day the engine surfaces ideas with a clear plan — entry, target, exit, timeframe — and a one-line **Why now**. A beginner sees what to do and why it matters today, not a wall of charts."

## Beat 3 — Bull vs Bear (1:15–2:15) · *Why is it different / trustworthy?*
Scroll to the Bull-vs-Bears card. Say: "This is the flagship. Every idea shows **both sides** — why bulls like it, why bears worry — and then where ArthOS lands and why. ChatGPT gives you a confident paragraph; ArthOS gives you the case *against* its own call. That's the difference between a chatbot and a copilot you can trust."

## Beat 4 — See the working (2:15–3:00) · *Why is it trustworthy?*
Expand "See the working". Say: "Nothing is a black box. This is the actual audit trail — every signal the engine weighed, what helped the ranking, what hurt it, what it set aside, and how the call was trimmed. An investor can verify the reasoning end to end."

## Beat 5 — Track Record Integrity + Reflection (3:00–4:00) · *Why is it trustworthy / why will users stay?*
Open Track Record. Show the Integrity Card: "Real return, realized P/L, drawdown, win-rate — all computed from real paper trades. And we **refuse to show a win-rate until 10 ideas resolve** — honesty is structural." Then the Reflection Loop: "When an idea closes, the system grades itself: what we expected, what happened, what we learned. That loop is why users come back — they watch the copilot, and themselves, get better."

## Beat 6 — Methodology workflow (4:00–4:40) · *Why is it difficult to copy?*
Open Methodology, show the Workflow diagram. Say: "One pipeline, end to end — market data, analysis, both sides, a recommendation, your paper portfolio, an honest track record, and reflection. The moat isn't one model — it's the **honest, beginner-safe loop** and the **per-user proof that compounds**. A competitor can copy a screen; they can't copy a year of a user's audited track record, or the discipline of showing the downside on every call."

## Beat 7 — Close (4:40–5:00) · *recap the five whys*
"Exists to make investing learnable. Different because it shows both sides and the working. Trustworthy because every number is real and gated. Users stay because the loop makes them better. Hard to copy because the moat is honesty + per-user proof that accrues over time — paper-only by construction, no regulatory exposure."

## The five answers, distilled
- **Why exists:** beginners can't learn to invest from trader tools — ArthOS teaches by doing, safely.
- **Why different:** both-sides reasoning + a visible audit trail, not a confident paragraph.
- **Why trustworthy:** real, gated, auditable numbers; shows the downside; paper-only.
- **Why users stay:** the reflection loop + a personal track record that compounds.
- **Why hard to copy:** the honest beginner-safe loop and per-user proof — not a model anyone can clone.

## Mobile note
The same flow works on a phone — Landing, Bull/Bear, Integrity Card, Reflection, and the Workflow diagram all stack cleanly at 391px (verified this sprint). For a phone demo, lead with Bull vs Bear (Beat 3) — it is the most striking single mobile screen.
