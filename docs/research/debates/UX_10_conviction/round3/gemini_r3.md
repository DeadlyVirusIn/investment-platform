## Final answers per question

### Q1. Conviction language
Use a **5-verb intent system**: `INITIATE` (start new), `ACCUMULATE` (scale in), `HOLD` (stay), `TRIM` (scale out), `EXIT` (terminate). Eliminate "BUY" and "SELL" to break the casino-transaction mental model. Verbs must be treated as **Status Badges**, not buttons. They are desaturated labels that signal the engine's current stance within a durable thesis.

### Q2. Confidence system
Adopt the **Confluence Matrix**. Replaces numeric scores with a 2x2 grid that maps **Freshness** (Opus) against **Evidence Confluence** (Gemini). The matrix cells are segmented by **F/T/M** (Fundamental, Technical, Macro) pillars. A "Confirmed" thesis is one where 2+ pillars align and data is <48h old. A "Holding" thesis has aligned pillars but stale data. No 0.82. No stars.

### Q3. ActionCard density
The **ActionCard** is a "Thesis Atom." Scan order: **Identity ($TICKER) → Freshness/Matrix → Invalidation Level → Current Intent (Verb) → Thesis Lead (1 sentence) → CTA (Cockpit).** Invalidation MUST appear above targets. Hide sparklines and social noise. Use monospace for all price levels to signal technical precision.

### Q4. Overview hero
The Hero is a **Strategist’s Brief**, not a "Hot Picks" list. It shows: **Regime Status** (e.g., "Market Regime: SELECTIVE") + **Active Thesis Counts** + **Top 3 Decision Briefs**. It must be capable of a "Quiet Day" state: "Regime: NOISY. No new entries recommended." This builds trust by demonstrating AI restraint.

### Q5. Stock detail (Research Cockpit)
The Cockpit is the **"War Room."** First paint: **Thesis Storyline + Invalidation Map + Bear Case.** The Bear Case is a "First-Class Citizen"—if the AI cannot articulate a credible reason why it's wrong, the thesis cannot move to "Confirmed" status. Include a "Decision Log" showing every time the AI changed its mind on this ticker.

### Q6. Options experience
Options are **"Engineered Structures,"** not "Bets." Use the verb `STRUCTURE` only. Every card must show **Max Loss** and **Probability of Profit (POP)** before showing Max Gain. Options are gated: they cannot be recommended without a linked, confirmed Equity Thesis. Defined-risk spreads are the default; naked premium is hidden behind advanced toggles.

### Q7. Trust safeguards
Implement 12 enforceable **Engineering Invariants**: (1) No card without Invalidation, (2) No card without Horizon, (3) Invalidation-above-Target, (4) Bear-case-mandated for top-tier conviction, (5) Action Scarcity (max 3 in hero), (6) Evidence Decay (visual fading), (7) No celebratory motion, (8) Wait-equal-to-Act affordance, (9) Snooze per thesis, (10) Action Ledger per ticker, (11) Decision Rest after 4 cards opened, (12) Saturation cap ≤ 50%.

### Q8. Visual system
**Quiet Luxury.** Palette: Graphite, Slate, desaturated Indigo (#3F51B5), and Amber. Typography: `Inter` for prose, `JetBrains Mono` for data. Sharp 2px borders, no large gradients, no glassmorphism. Visuals should feel "earned"—a glyph only appears when evidence exists. The "Staleness" of a card is reflected in its opacity (faded = stale).

### Q9. AI reasoning drawer
A single-scroll **Evidence Ledger**. Contains: **Thesis Recap → Drivers vs. Counters → What Changed (Timeline) → Calibration (Hit Rate) → Compared Candidates (Selectivity).** Use "Evidence Chips"—inline links to raw filings or data points—so the user can verify the AI's claims. No internal prompt traces or raw probabilities.

### Q10. Biggest failure modes
1. **Signal Spam:** Fixed by Hero frequency caps and Regime-gating.
2. **Fake Confidence:** Fixed by Confluence Matrix and numeric abolition.
3. **Action Bias:** Fixed by "Quiet Day" states and "Wait-equal-to-Act" visual balance.
4. **Hindsight Bias:** Fixed by "Thesis Versioning" and immutable decision logs.

### Q11. Final synthesis
The UX-10 "Conviction Engine" succeeds by **slowing the user down**. It transforms the user from a "Trader" reacting to signals into a "Strategist" managing a portfolio of theses. By prioritizing the **Bear Case**, **Invalidation Levels**, and **Evidence Freshness**, we build a premium, trustworthy product that differentiates itself from the "Casino" culture of modern retail fintech.
